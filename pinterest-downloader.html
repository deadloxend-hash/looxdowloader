Pinterest Video & Image Pins

Pinterest Downloader

Pinterest saves ideas as Pins, and a Pin may contain an image, a video, or a collection of visual details. This tool is intended for public Pin URLs rather than Pinterest boards and search pages.

Find media from a public Pinterest link

Public content only • No account password needed • Results depend on the source

How to use the Pinterest downloader

  1. Open a public Pinterest post or video and use its Share or Copy link option.
  2. Paste the link into the field above, then select “Find available media.”
  3. Review the returned file types and open or save only the files you are permitted to keep.

About Pinterest Video & Image Pins downloads

A photo Pin may provide an image instead of a video. File extensions and original dimensions depend on the Pin and available source data. For video Pins, some files may be viewable rather than directly downloadable.

Getting the right link

Open the exact Pin before copying its URL. A link to a board, profile or search result usually does not identify one downloadable piece of media.

Why a Pinterest video might not download

A link may fail if it leads to private or deleted content, has expired, or is blocked by the source platform. Check that the address opens the intended media publicly in your browser. The available formats can also change when a platform updates its delivery method. LOOX Media Grab does not bypass logins, paywalls, or access restrictions.

Pinterest downloader FAQs

Can this download Pinterest pictures?

Yes, public Pins may return image files as well as videos, depending on what the service can resolve.

Why does my board link not work?

Use the URL of an individual Pin; a whole board is not a single supported media item.
