Search YouTube

Look up videos, music, Shorts, channels, playlists or live streams by words rather than pasting a video URL. Results link to YouTube for playback.

Find content by title, artist or keyword

How to search YouTube

  1. Enter a video title, artist, creator name, or keyword.
  2. Choose a result type, such as Videos, Music or Shorts.
  3. Open a matching result to watch it on YouTube. Search results do not create a local download.

Finding the right result

If the title is common, add the artist, a channel name or a descriptive phrase. Switching from Videos to Music can be useful when a track also appears as interviews and clips. Results may differ from YouTube's own ranking and can change over time.

Search versus download

This page helps you discover content by keyword. To check whether a specific video has accessible media formats, use the YouTube downloader page and paste a direct watch or Shorts link. Respect the creator's rights and YouTube's terms.

FAQ

Can I search for YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Select Shorts from the result-type menu, then enter a topic or creator name.

Does searching download a video?

No. The search returns video links and metadata; it does not save videos to your device.
