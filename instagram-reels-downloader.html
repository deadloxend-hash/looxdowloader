Instagram Reels, Posts & Carousels

Instagram Downloader

Instagram links can point to a Reel, a single photo, or a carousel containing several items. This page focuses on those different post types instead of treating every Instagram link as a video.

Find media from a public Instagram link

Public content only • No account password needed • Results depend on the source

How to use the Instagram downloader

  1. Open a public Instagram post or video and use its Share or Copy link option.
  2. Paste the link into the field above, then select “Find available media.”
  3. Review the returned file types and open or save only the files you are permitted to keep.

About Instagram Reels, Posts & Carousels downloads

When a public post can be processed, you may receive video or image links. The resolution and format depend on the original post and what the media service can return; private stories and private-account posts are not supported.

Getting the right link

Use the post's share menu to copy its direct link. Profile links, hashtag pages and links to your inbox are not downloadable posts.

Why a Instagram video might not download

A link may fail if it leads to private or deleted content, has expired, or is blocked by the source platform. Check that the address opens the intended media publicly in your browser. The available formats can also change when a platform updates its delivery method. LOOX Media Grab does not bypass logins, paywalls, or access restrictions.

Instagram downloader FAQs

Can I save an Instagram carousel?

When the service can access the public post, its available media items may be shown separately. Private or restricted carousels cannot be retrieved.

Does this work for Instagram Stories?

This tool is designed for supported public post, Reel and TV URLs, not stories or private accounts.
