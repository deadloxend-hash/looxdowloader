Frequently asked questions

Practical answers about supported websites, formats, permissions, browser downloads, and common failures.

Links, downloads and video formats

How do I download a video?

Open the page for the correct platform, paste a direct public post or video URL, then select Find available media. If files are returned, open or save the option permitted by the source and your rights. See our full guides.

Why doesn't the download button work?

Verify the post is public and not deleted, copy the individual media URL rather than a profile or board link, and check your internet connection. Some source sites block retrieval or change their systems. Use the troubleshooting checklist.

Can I download private or password-protected videos?

No. We don't request passwords or bypass login, paywall or privacy settings. The media must be publicly accessible and supported.

Can I choose 1080p or 4K?

The source decides which formats are available. Higher quality isn't always available, and some YouTube quality options separate video from audio. We don't manufacture a resolution that doesn't exist.

What is the difference between MP4 and MP3?

MP4 is a container commonly used for video (and sometimes audio); MP3 is an audio format. Merely renaming a file doesn't convert it. Learn about formats.

Does YouTube MP3 always work?

No. A song or audio-only stream must be offered and accessible. This tool doesn't guarantee MP3 conversion. Read our YouTube audio guide.

Can I download YouTube Shorts?

A public Shorts URL can be checked on the YouTube tool page. As with long videos, file formats and access vary. See the Shorts guide.

What are .m3u8 and HLS playlists?

HLS is a streaming format consisting of a playlist and video segments. A .m3u8 link is not a self-contained MP4 download. RuTube often returns such links. Read the format guide.

Why do some videos have no sound?

Some platforms, especially YouTube, serve high-resolution video and audio in separate streams. Choose a combined format if one is available; this tool does not merge files.

Does a downloaded video lose its watermark?

Not necessarily. We cannot promise watermark removal or alteration. The output depends on the source and permitted media.

Privacy and device support

Do I need to register or install an app?

No account or additional installation is required to visit the site and use supported public links. The tool runs in a modern mobile or desktop browser.

Do you keep or host the original videos?

Our downloader backend requests metadata or file links from a service and returns results. It doesn't run a public user-media library. Third parties may process requests, and normal server logs and analytics may exist. Read the privacy policy.

Can I download videos on iPhone or Android?

Yes, on many supported browsers. How files are saved or opened depends on your device, browser and media format. Safari may preview content rather than saving it directly.

Is bulk downloading or scraping supported?

No bulk downloader or automated scraping tool is provided by the interface. Avoid sending high-volume automated requests.

Rights, support and safety

Is it legal to download anything I find online?

No. Public access does not automatically give permission to copy or redistribute material. Save only work you own or content you have rights or authorization to use, and follow applicable laws and platform terms.

How can a copyright owner contact you?

Visit Copyright & DMCA for a rights-reporting process, or email deadloxend@gmail.com.

Who runs LOOX Media Grab?

LOOX Media Grab is an independent downloader and guide website. It is not affiliated with the named social-media platforms. Read more about the project.
