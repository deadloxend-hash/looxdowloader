Privacy Policy

An overview of data used by our browser-based media tools. Updated November 4, 2026.

Information you choose to provide

When you paste a public media URL and select a downloader, that URL and the selected platform and format preferences are transmitted to our backend. The backend may send the URL to the third-party media processing provider to resolve available media. Only submit public URLs you are authorized to use. We don't request your social-network account password.

Analytics and site measurement

The homepage uses Google Analytics and Statcounter scripts. These third-party services may collect usage and technical information such as visited pages, browser characteristics, approximate location and IP-related data according to their own policies. The exact cookies and retention rules depend on those providers. Browser privacy controls and blocking settings may affect tracking.

Ratings and feedback

Where a ratings component is available, the site can record submitted star ratings, comments and a locally stored visitor identifier used for rating-related features. Please avoid putting personal or sensitive information in a public rating comment. Emails sent to support arrive in the mailbox used for replies.

Stored preferences

Your chosen theme, custom accent and navigation language are saved in your browser's local storage. These are local display preferences; you can clear them by clearing browser site data or by resetting the theme on Settings.

Server logs and data transfers

Hosting and infrastructure providers can record standard request metadata, which may include URLs, IP addresses, timestamps and security logs. Media files or URLs may be served from external hosts, so opening a returned link may connect your browser directly to a third party. We cannot control the privacy practices of the original platforms or third-party providers.

Data retention and choices

Retention periods depend on the services processing the information and any operational needs. We do not promise a specific deletion period without a verifiable backend policy. To request help with identifiable data you submitted, contact deadloxend@gmail.com. You can also clear local browser preferences and adjust privacy settings or tracking protection in your browser.

Policy changes

We may update this page when site functionality or service providers change. The date at the top identifies the latest editorial update. Questions can be sent through the Contact page.
