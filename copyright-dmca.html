Copyright & rights reporting

Respecting creators is part of responsible media use. Use this page to report concerns about links or website materials.

What the service does

LOOX Media Grab provides URL-based media discovery and downloader tools for publicly accessible sources. It does not give visitors permission to copy a work merely because a download link can be found. Third-party platforms may control original media availability and access rules.

Report a copyright concern

Send an email to deadloxend@gmail.com with the subject “Copyright / DMCA report”. Please include:

  1. Your name and a way to contact you.
  2. Identification of the original copyrighted work or works you own or represent.
  3. The specific LOOX Media Grab page URL or affected content and enough detail to locate it.
  4. A brief explanation of your rights and why you believe the material or link is infringing.
  5. A good-faith statement that the complained-of use is not authorized by the copyright owner, agent or law.
  6. A statement, under penalty of perjury where required, that the report is accurate and you are authorized to act for the owner, with a physical or electronic signature.

What happens next

We can review reports concerning pages or functionality we operate and take appropriate action when a valid complaint identifies material under our control. For content hosted by YouTube, Facebook, TikTok or another platform, you may also need to use that platform's reporting process. We cannot remove a post from a website we do not operate.

Misidentification and responses

If you believe content was mistakenly identified, email the same contact with the affected URL and supporting context. Do not submit false notices. Reporting procedures depend on the applicable jurisdiction; this page is not legal advice.
