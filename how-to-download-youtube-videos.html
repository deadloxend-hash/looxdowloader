How to check YouTube video downloads

A practical guide to valid YouTube watch URLs and available formats, without promising every video can be saved.

Before you begin

Only download YouTube material when you own it, have permission, or YouTube otherwise authorizes the download. YouTube offers official offline features in some locations and plans. A third-party tool does not override platform rules or copyright law.

1. Open the video

Find the video you are permitted to save in YouTube. A video page normally uses a youtube.com/watch?v=... address, while a short link may start with youtu.be/.

2. Copy the direct link

Use Share → Copy link or copy the browser address. A link to the YouTube homepage, search results or a channel does not point to one video.

3. Check available formats

Go to the YouTube downloader, paste the public link, and select Find available media. If source formats are returned, inspect their labels rather than assuming every result is an MP4 with audio.

4. Review the result

Choose a permitted format. Your browser may open the file directly rather than download it, and some resolutions may be video-only. Don't distribute material without the necessary rights.

Common questions

Is 1080p always available?

No. A resolution appears only when the source offers it. High-resolution streams can require separate audio.

Can I use a private video link?

No. Restricted content is outside the supported public-link workflow.
