LOOX MediaGrab does not host, store, copy, or redistribute copyrighted media files on its servers. All media items displayed or extracted through this service are retrieved directly from third-party Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) belonging to their respective social networks.

We respect intellectual property rights. Users are solely responsible for ensuring they have appropriate legal rights, permissions, or creator consents before saving or reusing any content. LOOX MediaGrab strictly forbids the use of its service for copyright infringement, unauthorized commercial redistribution, or harvesting private content.

DMCA Takedown Requests

If you are a copyright owner and believe your intellectual property has been improperly linked or indexed, please contact us with verifiable documentation.
