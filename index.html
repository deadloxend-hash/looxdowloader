Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about using Loox

Which platforms does Loox support?

The interface offers Instagram Reels, X, Pinterest, and TikTok. A platform will only work when its server-side extraction integration is implemented and operating correctly.

Why did my link fail?

The URL may be invalid, the post may be unavailable, the platform may have changed, or the backend may not support that platform or content type yet. Try a valid public link later if the service is temporarily unavailable.

Can I download private content?

No. Loox is intended for permitted public links and must not be used to bypass private accounts, login requirements, or access controls.

Do I need permission to save or share media?

You are responsible for having the rights or permission required for your intended use. Downloading a public post does not automatically give you permission to repost or commercially use it.

Is Loox connected to Instagram, X, TikTok, or Pinterest?

No. Loox is an independent project and is not endorsed by or affiliated with these platforms. Their names and logos belong to their respective owners.

Does Loox store downloaded files?

This depends on the backend implementation. Before launch, configure and document how submitted URLs, temporary files, and server logs are handled, and remove temporary media when it is no longer needed.
