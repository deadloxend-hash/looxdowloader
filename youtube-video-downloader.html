YouTube Downloader

YouTube can provide separate video-only and audio-only formats, especially at higher resolutions. A file offered by this tool may therefore not contain both picture and sound. This page helps you understand those format differences.

How to use the YouTube downloader

  1. Open a public YouTube post or video and use its Share or Copy link option.
  2. Paste the link into the field above, then select “Find available media.”
  3. Review the returned file types and open or save only the files you are permitted to keep.

About YouTube Videos & Shorts downloads

Depending on the source, the service may return MP4, WebM or audio formats. Some available variants require a compatible player or additional merging; this page does not perform audio/video muxing or promise offline playback.

Getting the right link

Paste a direct YouTube watch link, a youtu.be short link, or a Shorts URL. Only download content you own, have permission to save, or are otherwise authorized to download under YouTube's terms.

Why a YouTube video might not download

A link may fail if it leads to private or deleted content, has expired, or is blocked by the source platform. Check that the address opens the intended media publicly in your browser. The available formats can also change when a platform updates its delivery method. LOOX Media Grab does not bypass logins, paywalls, or access restrictions.

YouTube downloader FAQs

Why does a high-resolution YouTube file have no sound?

Some higher-quality streams contain video only, while audio is delivered separately. Choose a combined file if available.

Are all YouTube videos downloadable?

No. Private, restricted or unavailable videos and unsupported formats may not return a usable file. Always follow the platform's terms.
