Identify a Song From a Clip

Heard a song in a clip but cannot remember its name? A direct link to a public audio or video file can be analyzed to look for a matching track. This tool is for identification, not music downloading.

Recognize music from a direct file link

How song identification works

  1. Find a direct, publicly accessible HTTPS URL pointing to audio or video media.
  2. Paste it into the input and select Identify song.
  3. When a match is available, review the song title, artist and other available track information.

What kind of URL should I use?

A direct audio or video file link, such as a publicly accessible MP3, M4A or MP4, is the right input. A typical Instagram, YouTube or TikTok post URL is a web page—not necessarily a direct media file—so it may not work. The recognition service analyzes audio rather than downloading the song.

If the song cannot be recognized

Speech, loud background noise, very short clips or unknown recordings may prevent a match. Try a longer section that clearly contains the music. There is no guarantee that every track can be identified.

FAQ

Can this identify songs in video files?

It can attempt recognition from accessible video media URLs when the service can extract audio.

Does this download MP3 music?

No. This is an identification tool. It returns song details when a match is found.
